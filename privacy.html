Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy describes how the Night Owl Publisher app ("the app"), operated by the owner of the YouTube channel The Night Owl Jazz Club, handles data.

What the app does

The app is used only by the channel owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services and to set video titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails.

Data we access

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses the owner's YouTube channel in order to upload and update the owner's own videos. The app does not access data of any other user.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app does not use cookies or analytics.

YouTube API Services and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Deleting data

Revoking access in Google settings immediately stops the app. Locally stored tokens are deleted by removing the app's credentials.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please contact us through the channel's About page.
